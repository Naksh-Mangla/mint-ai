Q1 1-Mark Definition
1 Mark

Define the primary physiological role of chlorophyll in plant chloroplasts.

Model Answer & Grading Rubric
Expected Answer:

Chlorophyll is a photosynthetic pigment located in chloroplast thylakoid membranes that absorbs light energy (primarily blue and red wavelengths) and converts it into chemical energy (ATP/NADPH) during photosynthesis.

States absorption of light energy and conversion into chemical energy 1.0 Mark

Q2 3-Mark Differentiation
3 Marks

Distinguish between aerobic and anaerobic respiration in living organisms (give 3 distinct comparative points).

Model Answer & Grading Rubric
Expected Answer:

1. Oxygen Requirement: Aerobic requires molecular oxygen; anaerobic occurs in the absence of oxygen.
2. Energy Yield: Aerobic yields high energy (36-38 ATP); anaerobic yields low energy (2 ATP).
3. End Products: Aerobic produces CO2 and H2O; anaerobic produces lactic acid or ethanol + CO2.

Point 1: Oxygen dependence 1.0 Mark
Point 2: Net ATP yield comparison 1.0 Mark
Point 3: End metabolic products 1.0 Mark

Q3 5-Mark Long Answer
5 Marks

Describe the structural compartmentalization of a mitochondrion and explain how each structure contributes to cellular respiration.

Model Answer & Grading Rubric
Expected Answer:

Mitochondria have an outer membrane, inner membrane, cristae, and matrix. Outer membrane contains porins. Inner membrane houses ATP synthase and electron transport chain. Cristae maximize surface area for phosphorylation. Matrix contains Krebs cycle enzymes, ribosomes, and mtDNA.

Introduction and outer membrane permeability 1.0 Mark
Cristae structure and surface area role 1.5 Marks
Inner membrane electron transport chain mechanism 1.5 Marks
Matrix contents (Krebs enzymes, mtDNA) & summary 1.0 Mark
